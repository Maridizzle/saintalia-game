## Origin

### The Opening (both)
*Origin -- both sides*

The opening fires simultaneously on both sides of the veil. Two separate people. Two separate moments of wrongness. One accidental connection.

FANTASY SIDE -- TYVIAN:
1. The world blinks black for an instant -- not a long blink, barely a fraction of a second. The darkness is something vast and deliberate passing very close.
2. The air goes wrong. A whoosh, like the room forgot to exist for a moment. When it remembered, Tyvian was slightly less than they were before. Something was taken.
3. The holographic terminal flickers. The light it throws is the wrong color. The signal it is reaching for comes from somewhere the terminal was never built to touch.
4. A message forms on the display -- character by character -- from somewhere that should not exist.

Mechanical Effect: Each veil slip saps energy -- a physical drain that is cumulative. This happens every time something slips through.

REALITY SIDE -- SASHA:
1. Sasha is alone, late, working on the commissioned mural.
2. Takes a progress photo to document the work.
3. Looks at the photo. Something is in the upper area that was not there. A creature. Partially obscured. One eye, open, dark, pointed directly at the camera.
4. Looks up at the ceiling. Nothing. Looks at the phone. Still there.
5. Panics. Tries to text their colleague -- a monsterologist.
6. Gets the number wrong. The message goes to Tyvian's terminal in Saintalia.
7. Includes an accidental photo attachment -- arrives heavily corrupted. Just enough to confirm something exists. Not enough to see the mural clearly.

THE CONNECTION: Neither can hang up. The veil did not engineer this. The organization did not plan it. It just happened.

### The Veil (both)
*Living primordial organism*

The veils are living primordial organisms -- beings of incomprehensible age and scale whose entire existence is to separate and contain the infinite universes from one another.

They exist interconnected, like fungus or mycelium, threading through the fabric of reality. Each veil is aware. Each is in communication with the others through the network they form.

WHAT IS HAPPENING TO THIS ONE: Something more insidious has been conducting experiments on them -- fusing them together into chimera. This one failed. It is dying. As it dies, the separation between Saintalia and the modern waking world is dissolving.

COMMUNICATION: Almost too much for the mortal psyche to bear. Contact feels like standing inside a thunderstorm that is also a library that is also a scream.

PHYSICAL SYMPTOMS -- In Saintalia: sky moves wrong, rivers taste of iron, stars move backward after midnight, doors that were not there yesterday. In the Waking World: cameras capture things that are not there, signals go to impossible destinations, reflections slightly delayed.

VEIL CHIMERA: What results when two living veils are fused. The fusion is the same process the entity uses to harvest cosmic energy. When two veils fuse and die together, they release harvestable cosmic energy. The entity collects this and forges a new universe -- a process it is addicted to.

**Links:** The Entity, The Opening

### The Entity (both)
*Universe-forging addict*

The organization is cover for a cosmic entity. This entity manipulates modern technology through human hands. It cannot act directly in the physical world. It works through networks, data, technology.

It has been conducting the experiments on the veils. It is responsible for the veil-chimera. It destroys systems to repeat the harvest -- a process it has become addicted to.

**Links:** The Veil, The Opening

### The Organization (reality)
*Name not yet established*

A eugenics research company. Their stated and genuine mission is to isolate the fundamental biological mechanisms of life to develop the ultimately healthy human genome. They believe they discovered something extraordinary at their research location. They did not discover it. They were placed there.

The entity identified the crossover point beneath what is now the organization's building. It needed a well-funded, motivated, intellectually serious presence to execute the steps of veil fusion -- steps which mirror the fundamental processes of life and death at the atomic level.

The humans inside think they are doing legitimate work. They have no idea they are being used. The mural commission was a PR move -- pure corporate vanity. Sasha was hired through legitimate channels with no connection to the research divisions. The building was not chosen because of Sasha -- the location came first.

### The Soul Tagging (both)
*Locked*

BEAT: 
Layer: 2
Position: Immediately following The Branching Confessions
Type: Interactive dual-player exchange, brief
Sides: Both, simultaneous

REPLACES EARLIER TERM: "Soul Fusion" -- renamed to avoid collision 
with the endgame veil fusion in The Conscription ending. This is a 
smaller, permanent contact event, not a vast permanent merge.

PHYSICAL STAGING:
Both players are drawn toward the known crack. Not summoned, pulled, 
the way a body leans toward an open door in a cold room. On the 
artist's side the pull comes from the mural. On P2's side, from the 
fissure named in Layer 1. At the crossover point, the two become 
aware of each other's presence before contact.

CONTACT MECHANIC:
Described as two soap bubbles touching, not colliding. No violence 
in the physics of it. Surface tension holds two worlds apart and 
gives briefly at one point, the way two bubbles share a wall for a 
moment before separating.

THE EXCHANGE:
During contact, both players answer the same four questions. Each 
answer is chosen from three options: Complete Honesty, Partial 
Honesty, or Lie.

QUESTIONS (same for both players):
1. What were you told?
2. Why does it matter to you?
3. What are you afraid will happen if this gets out?
4. Do you trust the one on the other side of this with it?

SCORING:
Each player's four answers are tallied. Complete honesty scores high, 
partial honesty scores medium, lie scores low. Each player lands in 
one of two buckets: Mostly Honest or Mostly Guarded.

OUTCOME MAPPING:
Both Mostly Honest -> Both Share outcome (Chunk 4)
Both Mostly Guarded -> Neither Shares outcome (Chunk 5)
Artist Honest / P2 Guarded -> Only Artist Shares outcome (Chunk 6)
P2 Honest / Artist Guarded -> Only P2 Shares outcome (Chunk 7)

THE MARK:
Contact leaves a permanent physical mark, a knotwork tattoo, in a 
visible location on each player. The two tattoos are close enough in 
pattern to read as a matched pair but differ slightly, reflecting 
which side each player came from. Neither player initially knows 
what the mark is or that it functions as a tag.

FUNCTION OF THE MARK:
This is what makes both players targets of the entity's agents 
starting in Layer 3. Not chosen specifically, marked incidentally 
by the contact, and now traceable because of it.

VARIANT NARRATOR NOTES:
Both Honest -- contact feels mutual and complete, mark settles in 
fast, almost comfortable
Both Guarded -- contact feels forced and disorienting, mark rises 
jagged, unsettling to the player wearing it
One Honest / One Guarded -- the honest player's mark feels hollow, 
given without full return, the guarded player's mark feels stolen, 
taken without full consent

OPEN QUESTIONS:
- Where on the body does the tattoo appear for each player?
- Does the tattoo ever play a functional role later 
  (e.g. burns, glows, aches near the entity)?
- Do the four questions get asked in a fixed order or does the 
  player choose the order?
